Sources, method and limits

  • Data: NIST Standard Reference Database 12 (hydrogen equation of state), evaluated with CoolProp . Pressures 1–100 bar; temperatures 14–100 K at 0.5 K steps. Saturation temperatures are from the same equation.
  • Phase labels: "Liquid" below the saturation temperature at the chosen pressure, "Gas" above it, and "Supercritical" above 33.1 K and 12.96 bar. Above 12.96 bar there is no saturation line, so the label is "Fluid" below 33.1 K and "Supercritical" above it.
  • Not modelled: metastable or two-phase (flashing) states, and any difference between ortho and para hydrogen composition. The NIST equation used here is for pure hydrogen, so a tank's real liquid may differ slightly from these values.
  • Uncertainty: the NIST equation is accurate to a small fraction of a percent for these properties in the range shown; the page does not add measurement uncertainty.
